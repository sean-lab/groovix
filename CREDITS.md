# Sound credits

The recorded instruments in `samples/` were recorded by the VSCO 2 Community Edition,
Karoryfer Samples, the University of Iowa Electronic Music Studios and Freesound contributors,
and are used under the [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/) license.
The files come from [nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments](https://github.com/nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments),
cut to at most 4 seconds, faded out, mixed to mono and re-encoded as 64 kbit/s MP3 for this app.

| Folder | Instrument | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `piano` | Piano | VSCO 2 |
| `guitar` | Acoustic guitar | University of Iowa EMS |
| `eguitar` | Electric guitar | Karoryfer Samples |
| `bass` | Electric bass | Karoryfer Samples |
| `organ` | Pipe organ | VSCO 2 |
| `strings` | Cello (low notes) and violin (high notes) | Freesound 12408 (flcellogrl) and VSCO 2 |
| `flute` | Flute | VSCO 2 |
| `sax` | Saxophone | Karoryfer Samples |
| `trumpet` | Trumpet | VSCO 2 |
| `harp` | Harp | VSCO 2 |
| `xylo` | Xylophone | VSCO 2 |

All synthesizer and drum sounds are generated live with the Web Audio API; they use no samples.
